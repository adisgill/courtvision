## 6. Agreement with NBA.com's ratings

NBA.com's official offensive, defensive and net rating come from the advanced box score in `kaggle.team_statistics_extended`, possession-weighted over each regular season and taken relative to league average (892 team-seasons). Our parts are converted to points per 100 possessions. *Plain sum* adds them with no fitting, as the spec intends: the parts are in points, so a plain sum should track the NBA's rating. *Fitted* lets a regression reweight them; the Four Factors (relative to league) are fitted the same way, for reference.
