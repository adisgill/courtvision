## 6. Agreement with NBA.com's ratings

Our parts are converted to points per 100 possessions. *Plain sum* adds them with no fitting, as the spec intends: the parts are in points, so a plain sum should track the NBA's rating. *Fitted* lets a regression reweight them; the Four Factors (relative to league) are fitted the same way, for reference.
